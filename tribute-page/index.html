About Kabza De Small

Kabza De Small, whose real name is Kabelo Petrus Motha, is a South African DJ, record producer and record label owner. He is widely recognised as one of the major figures who helped shape and popularise Amapiano music.

Kabza De Small was born on 27 November 1992 in Mpumalanga, South Africa. His musical journey developed through house music, piano melodies and electronic production.

His breakthrough came with the song Umshove in 2018. The song helped him gain wider attention and introduced his sound to more listeners.

Kabza De Small is also one half of the famous Scorpion Kings alongside DJ Maphorisa. Their collaboration has played an important role in the growth of Amapiano.

Through his production style, collaborations and PianoHub record label, Kabza has helped bring South African dance music to audiences around the world.

Recent Achievements

2023 — Isimo

Kabza De Small released the extended play Isimo, featuring the popular song Imithandazo.

2024 — Red Bull Symphonic

His Red Bull Symphonic performance with Ofentse Pitse and a symphonic orchestra sold out rapidly and highlighted the versatility of his music.

2025 — Bab’Motha

Kabza De Small released the studio album Bab’Motha, adding another important project to his growing discography.

2025 — Kings Will Rise

Kabza De Small and DJ Maphorisa continued their Scorpion Kings journey with the album Kings Will Rise.

2026 — New Music

Kabza De Small continued releasing and appearing on new music projects, including collaborations and material connected with his upcoming work.
